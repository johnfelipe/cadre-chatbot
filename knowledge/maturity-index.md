# AI Maturity Index

Source: Cadre AI take-home brief v1.1 (seed); https://cadreai.com, https://portal.gocadre.ai/ai-maturity-index,
https://cadreai.com/strategy (fetched 2026-09-24).

## Facts
- The AI Maturity Index is a Cadre AI offering that prospects commonly ask about.
- It scores a company across Cadre's eight-pillar framework for AI transformation. You get a grade in each
  area with clear explanations, plus actionable insights on how to improve. (source: https://cadreai.com)
- How to get scored: take the online assessment at https://portal.gocadre.ai/ai-maturity-index
  (the site's "Get Your AI Maturity Index" link). (source: https://cadreai.com)
- It is free and takes about 10 minutes, for you and your team. (source: https://portal.gocadre.ai/ai-maturity-index)
- To start you enter company name, company website, your name and work email; Cadre emails a 6-digit
  code to confirm it's you. (source: https://portal.gocadre.ai/ai-maturity-index)
- The eight pillars of AI transformation: (1) build a dedicated AI team, (2) deploy an AI command center
  (one company-wide platform such as ChatGPT, Copilot or Claude), (3) an AI-first culture shift,
  (4) a connected, AI-enabled tech stack, (5) an AI-healthy data assessment, (6) a framework for AI agent
  readiness, (7) departmental AI deep dives, (8) a 3-year AI vision. (source: https://cadreai.com/strategy)
