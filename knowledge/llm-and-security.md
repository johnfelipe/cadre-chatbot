# LLM selection and data security

Source: Cadre AI take-home brief v1.1 (seed); https://cadreai.com, https://cadreai.com/strategy,
https://cadreai.com/ai-engineering, https://cadreai.com/legal/privacy-policy,
https://cadreai.com/articles/cadre-ai-selected-as-an-official-openai-service-partner (fetched 2026-09-24).

## Facts
- Cadre AI partners with multiple model and cloud providers: OpenAI, Anthropic (Claude), Google,
  Microsoft, AWS, Salesforce, and Snowflake, plus OpenRouter for model access.
- That partner list means Cadre is not tied to a single model vendor.
- Cadre helps clients select and configure the LLM(s) that best fit their tech stack and business goals.
  (source: https://cadreai.com)
- Its "LLM Selection & Data Security" offering: pick the right LLM for your use cases, "black-box" your
  data so it's never used to train other models, stop employees sharing company secrets on personal LLMs,
  and get the whole team on secure, compliant AI tools. (source: https://cadreai.com/ai-engineering)
- It recommends one company-wide "AI command center" platform (e.g. ChatGPT, Copilot, Claude) to stop
  employees using personal accounts with company data. (source: https://cadreai.com/strategy)
- As an official OpenAI service partner, Cadre builds enterprise AI command centers that turn fragmented,
  risky AI usage into secure, centralized systems. (source: https://cadreai.com/strategy)
- The website privacy policy (personal information from the website and services) says data is kept
  2 years in practice, then deleted or anonymized; questions go to privacy@gocadre.ai.
  (source: https://cadreai.com/legal/privacy-policy)
