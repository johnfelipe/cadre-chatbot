## Facts
- The published industry list (use this one; it includes every industry the brief names):
  Professional Services, Private Equity (including lower middle market PE-backed companies), Real Estate,
  Financial Services, Mortgage & Lending, Construction, Retail & E-commerce, Manufacturing & Logistics,
  Hospitality. (source: https://cadreai.com/industries)
- The list is not exhaustive ("and more").
- For industries not listed, the site says Cadre has worked with companies "you wouldn't expect to use AI"
  and invites a conversation. (source: https://cadreai.com/industries)
- Cadre works with companies of all sizes. Best fit: businesses with manual workflows that get less
  efficient as they grow, often B2B and B2C services companies, plus PE-backed companies looking to
  grow EBITDA without adding headcount. (source: https://cadreai.com)
- Published case studies cover professional services, manufacturing & logistics, hospitality, real estate,
  and financial services / mortgage & lending (see case-studies.md). (source: https://cadreai.com/case-studies)

## How to answer
- Named industry (including close matches, e.g. hotels → Hospitality, lenders → Mortgage & Lending):
  confirm Cadre works with it.
- Industry not named: say it is not on the published list, that the list is not exhaustive, and suggest
  a strategist call to confirm fit. Do not claim experience in that industry.
