# Booking a strategy call

Source: Cadre AI take-home brief v1.1 (seed); https://cadreai.com, https://cadreai.com/contact,
https://cadreai.com/strategy, https://cadreai.com/leadership-facilitation (fetched 2026-09-24).

## Facts
- Prospects can book a strategy call with a Cadre AI strategist. It is the usual way to get started.
- Every "Talk to an AI Strategist" and "Get started" button on the site leads to the contact page,
  https://cadreai.com/contact, which has a contact form. (source: https://cadreai.com/strategy)
- The contact page also lists hello@gocadre.ai and (619) 324-3223. (source: https://cadreai.com/contact)
- Executive facilitation sessions are requested through the same contact page ("Schedule Executive
  Facilitation"). (source: https://cadreai.com/leadership-facilitation)

## How to answer
- Always call the `get_booking_link` tool to get the booking link. Never write a booking URL yourself.
- The link opens Cadre's contact form, not a calendar: the user requests the call there, or through the
  support email or phone above.

- [NOT PUBLISHED] A self-serve calendar link (no Calendly-style scheduler is linked on the site).
- [NOT PUBLISHED] Call length, cost (if any), and availability.
