## What the work covers overall
- Going department by department to identify high-ROI AI opportunities.
- Building workflows (workflow automation) and AI agents.
- Training teams so the changes actually stick.
- Departments covered on the site: sales, marketing, customer success, executive leadership, finance,
  operations, technology, legal. (source: https://cadreai.com/legal/privacy-policy)

## AI Strategy
- Run as a structured 45-day "AI Transformation Intensive" that ends in a prioritized roadmap.
  (source: https://cadreai.com/strategy)
- Four steps: discover use cases (interview teams, calculate ROI, prioritize), survey the landscape
  (existing tools, capabilities coming in 3-6 months), implement solutions (deploy, train, iterate),
  scale with confidence (expand to other departments, internal champions, a 3-year roadmap).
  (source: https://cadreai.com/strategy)
- Cadre says it does not deliver slide decks and walk away: it finds quick wins, builds the roadmap, then
  implements it. (source: https://cadreai.com/strategy)
- Built on the "8 Pillars of AI Transformation" (see maturity-index.md). (source: https://cadreai.com/strategy)

## AI Leadership & Facilitation
- Experiential sessions for executives and teams that combine technical implementation, behavioral
  science and executive coaching. (source: https://cadreai.com/leadership-facilitation)
- Mix: 30% best practices, 30% live problem-solving, 40% work on the client's real challenges;
  participants leave with 3-5 prioritized AI opportunities and an action plan.
  (source: https://cadreai.com/leadership-facilitation)
- Session formats: 2-Day Leadership Intensive, 1-Day Workshop, Half-Day Executive Session, 1-Hour Virtual
  Kickoff. Sessions are tailored per department. (source: https://cadreai.com/leadership-facilitation)

## AI Engineering
- Steps: understand the problem, pick the right approach (existing tools, workflow automation, or custom
  agents), integrate and automate across the tech stack via APIs. (source: https://cadreai.com/ai-engineering)
- Covers automating repetitive work, connecting disconnected systems, adding AI to workflows, and custom
  agents. (source: https://cadreai.com/ai-engineering)
- Cadre decides when to buy, when to automate and when to build custom; it builds custom only when there
  is no faster or smarter option. (source: https://cadreai.com)
- It works with a client's current tools, starting with a review of the tech stack and processes.
  (source: https://cadreai.com)

## AI Agents
- Three kinds: prompts & assistants, voice agents (intake, qualification, support, routing), and fully
  fledged agents that act across tools with guardrails and human oversight. (source: https://cadreai.com/agents)
- Example agents on the site: Review Responder, Sales Objection Coach, Help Desk Response Drafter,
  Ops Dispatcher, Due Diligence Analyst, Intake Qualifier, Discovery Call Deck Generator.
  (source: https://cadreai.com/agents)
