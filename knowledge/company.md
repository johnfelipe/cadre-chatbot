# Company overview

Source: Cadre AI take-home brief v1.1 (seed); https://cadreai.com, https://cadreai.com/about,
https://cadreai.com/contact, https://cadreai.com/legal/privacy-policy,

## Facts
- Cadre AI is an AI strategy and implementation consultancy.
- It helps businesses move from AI confusion to AI confidence. (source: https://cadreai.com)
- It works department by department to identify high-ROI AI opportunities, builds workflows and agents,
  and trains teams so the changes actually stick.
- What it does: AI strategy, workflow automation, AI agents, and leadership facilitation for businesses.
- It describes itself as a consultancy focused on using AI to drive revenue growth and improve EBITDA,
  helping companies scale with less overhead. (source: https://cadreai.com)
- Its three stated outcomes: drive revenue, increase profitability, elevate employees. (source: https://cadreai.com)
- Its approach is "Find. Prepare. Implement.": find the right problems, prepare people to lead change,
  implement solutions with measurable results. (source: https://cadreai.com)
- Clients range from lower middle market private equity-backed companies to professional services firms
  and financial services organizations.
- It describes its clients as mid-market and enterprise companies.
  (source: https://cadreai.com/articles/cadre-ai-selected-as-an-official-openai-service-partner)
- Key partners (brief): OpenAI, Anthropic (Claude), Google, Microsoft, AWS, Salesforce, Snowflake, plus
  OpenRouter for model access. The website's "Partnering with the best" logo strip shows Meta, OpenAI,
  Snowflake, Salesforce, Microsoft and AWS. (source: https://cadreai.com)
- Cadre AI was named one of the first Official Service Partners of OpenAI (announced January 2026).
  (source: https://cadreai.com/articles/cadre-ai-selected-as-an-official-openai-service-partner)
- Its own published figures: "100+ high-ROI use cases delivered across 50+ companies".
  (source: https://cadreai.com/about)
- Leadership team: Grayson Lafrenz (Founder / CEO), Keith Jensen (President), Riley Stricklin
  (Founder, Chief Strategy Officer), Wesley Gonzalez (COO), Nicole Kelley (CFO), Sarah McLoughlin
  (Chief Client Officer), Chad Lohrli (Founder, Chief AI Officer), Katie Daggett (VP of Client Strategy).
  (source: https://cadreai.com/about)
- Company values ("The Cadre Way"): growth mindset, extreme ownership, team first, scrappy.
  (source: https://cadreai.com/about)
- Legal name: AI Gurus LLC dba Cadre AI. (source: https://cadreai.com/legal/privacy-policy)
- Office: 3580 Carmel Mountain Rd, #150, San Diego, CA 92130. (source: https://cadreai.com/contact)
- Support contact: hello@gocadre.ai, (619) 324-3223. (source: https://cadreai.com/contact)
- Other public resources: case studies, articles (blog), the AI 2030 Podcast and 2030 Podcast, events,
  and a careers page. (source: https://cadreai.com)
